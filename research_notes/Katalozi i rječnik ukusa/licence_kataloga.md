> Method: official pages were fetched where possible. Many official sites returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher (IGDB docs, BGG, Discogs, MusicBrainz, Podcast Index), so some facts come from search-engine snippets of those official pages. ## Q1. Per-source table: licences, commercial use, attribution, rate limits, dumps, caching, contracts, ML/profiling limits

### Takeaway
Only a few sources are truly "free + commercial + storable": Wikidata (CC0), MusicBrainz core data (CC0), Discogs data dumps (CC0 part), and TVmaze (CC BY-SA, commercial OK with credit). Open Library and Podcast Index are open, but with rate-limit and "not a commercial backend" caveats. TMDB, Last.fm, IMDb, BGG, RAWG (free tier), MobyGames (free), Google Books (if you charge users), Hardcover (professional use) and JustWatch all require a contract or paid licence for a commercial app. Spotify, TMDB and BGG explicitly ban using their data to train ML/AI models, and that is the clause most likely to touch a "taste profile / similarity" feature.

### Cited Findings

**Music**
- MusicBrainz Live Data Feed: "Non-commercial / personal users may sign up and obtain a free access token"; commercial users "support our efforts financially"; tokens on a "sliding support scale for commercial use"; commercial sign-up is on metabrainz.org — [MusicBrainz docs: Live Data Feed](https://musicbrainz-docs-development.readthedocs.io/en/latest/products/live_data_feed.html); [MetaBrainz blog](https://blog.metabrainz.org/tag/logos); [MetaBrainz supporters](https://metabrainz.org/supporters). Concrete tier prices were not found.
- The CC0 vs CC BY-NC-SA split could not be fetched (the official page at musicbrainz.org/doc/About/Data_License returned an error). From background knowledge: core entities are CC0, while the "supplementary/derived" data (tags, ratings, annotations, edit history) are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 **[UNVERIFIED]** — [MusicBrainz Data License](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/About/Data_License).
- Cover Art Archive: "all images are copyrighted by their respective copyright owners"; joint project of the Internet Archive and MusicBrainz — [coverartarchive.org](https://coverartarchive.org/); [MusicBrainz: Cover Art Archive](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/Cover_Art_Archive).
- Discogs API Terms: CC0 data covers "release titles, notes, dates, format, track listings, barcodes and other identifiers, credits, versions, URL links…". Restricted Data gets "a limited, personal, non-sublicensable, non-transferable, non-exclusive, revocable license". Images count as CC0 only if the contributor released them that way. Monthly dumps are CC0 — [Discogs API Terms of Use](https://support.discogs.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009334593); [vinylanddata blog, 2019](https://vinylanddata.blogspot.com/2019/03/discogs-data-is-openmostly.html).
- Last.fm: "Use of the Last.fm Data is permitted solely for non-commercial purposes". Commercial use needs a commercial agreement (partners@last.fm) — [Last.fm API ToS](https://www.last.fm/api/tos).
- Spotify Developer Terms, "Version 10, effective as of 15 May, 2025": bans "using the Spotify Platform or any Spotify Content to train a machine learning or AI model"; "Do not store Spotify Content indefinitely"; may not "create compilations or databases of Spotify Content" except as strictly necessary; only temporary caching of metadata and cover art; no transfer to "ad network, ad exchange, data broker"; no selling of data; extended quota "no promise or guarantee" — [Spotify Developer Terms](https://developer.spotify.com/terms).
- Deezer: "We don't deliver any certification for developers… for commercial or non-commercial use"; some use is "not possible, unless there is a commercial agreement" — [Deezer developer FAQ](https://support.deezer.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360011538897-Deezer-FAQs-For-Developers); [Deezer guidelines](https://developers.deezer.com/guidelines).
- Apple Search API: "approximately 20 calls per minute (subject to change)"; for heavier needs, Enterprise Partner Feed — [Apple Performance Partners: Search API](https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api).

**Film/TV**
- TMDB API Terms (last updated 20 Oct 2023): commercial use requires a written agreement that "may involve fees"; use without one is a "material breach"; no caching longer than 6 months; logo must be less prominent than yours, plus a "not endorsed" notice; bans using TMDB to "train or validate machine learning or AI systems"; no derivatives — [TMDB API Terms of Use](https://www.themoviedb.org/api-terms-of-use); [TMDB API for Business](https://tmdb.org/api-for-business).
- Commercial price of $149/month for companies under $1M revenue; custom above that — [api.market blog (secondhand, unverified)](https://api.market/blog/sleeyax/entertainment/best-movie-apis).
- IMDb datasets: "personal and non-commercial use"; local copies allowed under IMDb terms; refreshed daily; source changed 18 Mar 2024; commercial: "Contact us" — [IMDb Non-Commercial Datasets](https://data.imdb.com/non-commercial-datasets/).
- TVmaze: CC BY-SA 4.0, data "can freely be used for any purpose, as long as TVmaze is properly credited"; ≥ 20 calls / 10 s per IP; images can be cached indefinitely; Enterprise option through sales — [TVmaze API](https://www.tvmaze.com/api).
- OMDb: free 1,000 req/day; Patreon ≥ $1; commercial status conflicting (one source says CC BY-NC 4.0) — [Zuplo comparison](https://zuplo.com/learning-center/best-movie-api-imdb-vs-omdb-vs-tmdb); [jentic OMDb](https://jentic.com/apis/omdbapi.com/omdbapi). The official omdbapi.com page was not checked.
- JustWatch: partners get a token "after the contract is concluded"; branded links required; Partner Dashboard terms ban redistribution — [JustWatch API docs](https://apis.justwatch.com/docs/api); [JustWatch partner terms](https://partners.justwatch.com/legal/termsofuse).
- Trakt: no official terms found; VIP-gated endpoints (HTTP 426) — [apis.guru Trakt](https://apis.guru/apis/trakt.tv).

**Books**
- Open Library: 1 req/s anonymous, 3 req/s identified (User-Agent + email); "Don't use the APIs to bulk download"; use monthly dumps; not intended as a "data backend for third-party services" or "high-traffic commercial infrastructure"; priority goes to mission-aligned projects; violations can mean "aggressive rate limiting or blocking" — [Open Library Developers API](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api).
- Google Books: "may not charge users for your application without a separate agreement or written permission from Google"; Google attribution required; may not "reorder or otherwise alter the results" — [Google Books API Terms](https://developers.google.com/books/terms); [Branding Guidelines](https://developers.google.com/books/branding).
- Hardcover (secondhand): professional use (charged for, incorporated, ad-supported) is restricted; strip aggregated ratings; no image hotlinking for commercial use — [bindery third-party-data.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vavallee/bindery/HEAD/docs/third-party-data.md).
- ISBNdb: plans of 1 / 3 / 5 req/s; Pro $99.99/month, Enterprise $299.99/month (secondhand) — [BookScouter](https://bookscouter.com/blog/book-databases/); [isbndb-client README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/isbndb-client@1.0.0/README.md).

**Podcasts**
- Podcast Index: software under the MIT License; whole database downloadable as SQLite; no explicit data licence ("free for any use" claim); ToS lives in GitHub Podcastindex-org/legal and was cut from 1,088 to 168 lines — [Wikipedia: Podcast Index](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Podcast_Index); [Typesense thread](https://threads.typesense.org/t/29411734/is-there-any-interest-in-fairly-large-publicly-available-dat); [Podnews](https://podnews.net/directory/company/podcast-index).
- Listen Notes: Free plan $0 with 300 requests/month; ToS says apps "must not pre-fetch, cache, index, or store any content on the server side" except id and pub_date; "Powered by Listen Notes" logo required; Enterprise allows storage. The pricing page contradicts this ("flexible server-side caching", "no logo requirements" on free) — [Listen Notes API terms](https://www.listennotes.com/api/terms); [Listen Notes pricing](https://www.listennotes.com/api/pricing/).

**Games**

**General / EU law**
- Sui generis database right (Directive 96/9/EC, Art. 7) protects against extraction or re-utilisation of a substantial part, judged qualitatively or quantitatively; "qualitatively substantial" is tied to the investment in obtaining, verifying or presenting the data (BHB) — [Kluwer Copyright Blog](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/copyright-blog/access-to-information-and-competition-concerns-enter-the-sui-generis-rights-infringement-test-the-cjeu-redefines-the-database-right/); [Directive text](https://legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1996/9/chapter/III/data.pdf).
- Innoweb (C-202/12): a meta-search engine that queried a protected database undermined the maker's right. CV-Online Latvia v Melons (C-762/19, 3 Jun 2021) added a test of whether the use harms the maker's investment or income — [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2021/uk/cv-online-latvia-cjeu-complicates-the-enforcement-of-database-rights); [Mishcon](https://mishcon.com/news/cjeu-ruling-narrows-the-scope-of-eu-database-rights); [Jacobacci](https://www.jacobacci-law.com/news-and-publications/cjeu-c-762-19-the-sui-generis-right-of-the-database-maker).
- Ryanair v PR Aviation (C-30/14, 2015): when a database is protected neither by copyright nor by the sui generis right, the maker can still restrict use by contract (website/API terms) — [IPPT PDF](https://www.ippt.eu/sites/ippt/files/2015/IPPT20150115_ECJ_Ryanair_v_PR_Aviation.pdf) (holding from background knowledge **[UNVERIFIED wording]**).
- The Commission is reviewing the Database Directive alongside the Data Act — [Kluwer](https://copyrightblog.kluweriplaw.com/2021/06/17/access-to-information-and-competition-concerns-enter-the-sui-generis-rights-infringement-test-the-cjeu-redefines-the-database-right/?output=pdf).

### Inferences
- **Safest base layer:** Wikidata (CC0) for cross-domain IDs, genres and links across all seven categories, plus MusicBrainz core (CC0) for music, Discogs dumps (CC0 fields) for music genres/styles, Open Library dumps for books, Podcast Index dump for podcasts, and TVmaze (BY-SA, commercial OK with credit) for TV. These cover item picking and basic metadata without a contract.
- **Contract needed before a commercial launch:** TMDB (films; the de facto standard), IGDB (games), BGG (board games), Last.fm (tags). Where TMDB/BGG/Spotify are used, computing taste similarity from their metadata is probably not "training an ML model". But if the app trains embeddings or any learned model on their content, that would likely breach the explicit AI/ML clauses. This is a legal grey zone; get a written clarification inside the commercial agreement.
- **MusicBrainz tags/genres:** if tags really sit in the CC BY-NC-SA part [unverified], using them in a commercial app needs MetaBrainz permission or a supporter agreement. Core genres linked to Wikidata may be a CC0 alternative.
- **TVmaze BY-SA:** ShareAlike could arguably extend to a derived database you publish, but not to private internal use. Its reach for an app's internal similarity database is ambiguous; flag it for legal review.
- **Images are the biggest risk across all sources:** cover art, posters and box art are almost always owned by labels, studios or publishers, whatever the metadata licence says. Hotlinking under each API's terms (TMDB, IGDB, Spotify) is the usual practice. Self-hosting copies needs care (Hardcover requires it for commercial use; TMDB allows 6-month caching).
- **Spotify/Apple/Deezer for item picking:** Spotify's ban on "compilations or databases of Spotify Content" and on indefinite storage makes it risky to store Spotify search results as the canonical item record. Storing only the Spotify ID alongside a MusicBrainz/Wikidata record is the common mitigation (inference, not legal advice).
- **EU database right:** systematically copying a substantial part of a non-CC0 catalogue (e.g. scraping TMDB or BGG) into your own database is risky in the EU even without the API terms. Under Ryanair, contractual terms bind even where no database right exists. CC0 dumps remove the issue.
